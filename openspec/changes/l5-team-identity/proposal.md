# L5 — more than one of us

## Why

A shared project cannot be owned by one person's keychain. Identity, consent and
contribution are the whole problem here, not the transport.

## What changes

- A person with a stable local identity, a device, and an explicit consent.
- Sharing a document deliberately, to a named person, never by link.
- Seeing who contributed what, on the object they touched.
- A refusal that is explicit, revocable and auditable.

## What it does not change

Nothing here starts a network service. The lot is scoped to identity and consent
so that L6 can move bytes without also inventing trust.
